### 🎮 **Core Gameplay Features**

1. **Multiple Lanes**:

   * The game has **five defined lanes** (`lanes = [100, 300, 500, 700, 900]`)
    that cars (player and enemies) can use.

2. **Enemy Vehicle Spawning**:

   * Enemy cars spawn randomly in available lanes and **move downward** toward the player.
   * Lanes are managed to **prevent overcrowding**, avoiding unfair obstacles.

3. **Player Car Movement**:

   * The player can move **left, right, up, and down** using arrow keys.
   * Movement is **limited to screen bounds** for better control.

4. **Jump Mechanic**:

   * Pressing the **spacebar triggers a jump**, useful to avoid enemy cars.
   * Includes **animation effects** (scaling + rotation) for visual feedback.
   * **Jumping avoids collisions**.

5. **Collectible Hearts**:

   * Randomly spawned **heart items** give **+5 score** when collected.
   * Helps boost score aside from surviving enemy cars.

---

### 📈 **Progression and Scoring**

6. **Score Tracking**:

   * Score increases by:

     * **+1** per enemy spawn (if survived),
     * **+5** per heart collected.
   * Displayed in the top-left.

7. **Leveling System**:

   * Game **levels up every 10 points**, with increasing difficulty.
   * On level up:

     * Enemy speed increases
     * Road scroll speed increases
     * Player car speed increases
     * Level threshold becomes harder (`+20` each time)

---

### 📋 **User Interface & Game Flow**

8. **Start Menu**:

   * Opening screen with **PLAY** and **EXIT** buttons.
   * Custom background and title using `opening_bg`.

9. **Pause Functionality**:

   * Pressing `P` pauses the game.
   * Displays pause screen with **RESUME** option and custom background.

10. **Game Over Screen**:

* Shown when player collides with an enemy (while not jumping).
* Displays **final score**.
* Prompts user to **enter their name**.
* Shows **leaderboard** (top 10 scores) fetched from a backend.
* Options to **REPLAY** or **EXIT** the game.

---

### 🧠 **Persistence and Backend Integration**

11. **Score Submission**:

* Sends score and name to a **Flask backend API** (`/submit`).

12. **Leaderboard Fetching**:

* Gets **top scores** from backend (`/leaderboard`).

13. **Local CSV Backup**:

* Saves score locally to `./Car_fun/scores.csv` as a fallback.

14. **Offline Score Loading**:

* Supports reading from local CSV when the backend is unavailable.

### 🖼️ **Graphics and Assets**

15. **Custom Visuals**:

* Custom backgrounds for each screen (intro, resume, game over).
* Different enemy vehicle sprites and road image.
* Player car and collectible heart icons.

16. **Animations**:
